2026 income tax bands

Tax is charged on taxable income, which is your gross income less the reliefs you qualify for. Each slice of taxable income is charged at that band’s rate, not the whole amount.

Reliefs and deductions we use

  • Rent relief Confirmed
    20% of your yearly rent, up to ₦500,000. Only if you pay rent.
  • Pension (employee contribution) Unverified
    Your own contribution, usually 8% of basic + housing + transport. You enter this amount; we do not estimate it.
  • National Housing Fund Unverified
    Usually 2.5% of basic salary, if deducted from your pay. You enter this amount; we do not estimate it.
  • Health insurance (NHIS) Unverified
    Your own contribution, if deducted from your pay. You enter this amount; we do not estimate it.
  • Life insurance premium Unverified
    Premium paid on your own or your spouse’s life. You enter this amount; we do not estimate it.

The Consolidated Relief Allowance no longer exists under the new rules. “Unverified” means the rule comes from commentary on the Act and is still being checked against the Act’s own text.

Not modelled

  • Interest on a loan for an owner-occupied home
  • Any minimum tax for individuals (not confirmed in the Act)
  • Minimum-wage exemption and other exemptions
  • Income other than regular employment income, such as business or investment income
  • State-level or employer-specific arrangements

Frequently asked questions

What are the personal income tax rates for 2026?

Under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, taxable income is charged in bands: first ₦800,000 at 0%, ₦800,000 to ₦3,000,000 at 15%, ₦3,000,000 to ₦12,000,000 at 18%, ₦12,000,000 to ₦25,000,000 at 21%, ₦25,000,000 to ₦50,000,000 at 23%, above ₦50,000,000 at 25%.

Is the first ₦800,000 really tax-free?

Yes. The first ₦800,000 of taxable income is charged at 0%. Because every taxpayer gets this, the old Consolidated Relief Allowance was removed.

What happened to the Consolidated Relief Allowance?

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 eliminated it from 1 January 2026. People who pay rent can claim rent relief instead. The calculator also shows what the old rules, including the allowance, would have charged, so you can compare.

How does rent relief work?

Rent relief is 20% of the rent you pay in a year, up to ₦500,000, whichever is lower. You must declare the rent you actually paid, and it can be verified, so keep your receipts or tenancy agreement.

Are pension, NHF, NHIS and life insurance deductible?

Commentary on the Act says employee pension contributions, National Housing Fund contributions, NHIS contributions and life insurance premiums can be deducted before tax. Sources differ on details, and we have not yet confirmed them against the Act’s text. So the calculator lets you enter the amounts from your payslip and marks each one as unverified. It never works them out from your salary.

Will this match my payslip?

Not necessarily. This is an estimate for regular employment income. Your employer’s payroll may treat allowances, benefits or reliefs differently, and some rules are not modelled (see the list on this page). Use it to understand and sanity-check, not as a substitute for your payslip or a tax adviser.

When did the new rules start?

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 was signed on 26 June 2025 and took effect on 1 January 2026, replacing the previous personal income tax rates and reliefs.

Does this site store my income?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. The “Copy link” button builds a link containing the figures you typed so you can share or save it; nothing is sent to us.
